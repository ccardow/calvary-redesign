# Brand Direction: Calvary Church Redesign
**From:** Sienna (Brand Director, FireBuilt Media)  
**For:** Calvary Church, Steinbach, MB  
**Date:** 2026-04-02

## Visual Identity Direction

### Core Concept: "Warm Welcome"
The current Squarespace feels stale, corporate-template, and disconnected from the community. We're pivoting to **warm, modern ministry** — like walking into a church where people actually smile at you.

### Color Palette
We'll use Calvary's existing identity but elevate it:

| Usage | Color | Hex |
|-------|-------|-----|
| **Primary** | Warm Navy (trust, calm) | `#1a2b4a` |
| **Accent** | Harvest Gold (warmth, light) | `#d4a045` |
| **Background** | Soft Cream (welcoming, clean) | `#faf8f5` |
| **Text** | Deep Charcoal (readable, not harsh) | `#2d2d2d` |
| **Muted** | Warm Gray (supporting text) | `#6b7280` |

### Typography
- **Headings:** `Inter` (weight 700-800) - modern, clean, authoritative but approachable
- **Body:** `Inter` (weight 400-500) - highly readable, pairs perfectly
- **Quote/Scripture:** `Georgia` (serif) - for biblical quotes, adds warmth
- **Fallbacks:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `sans-serif`

### Mood & Feel
- Photos of real congregation moments (not stock church stock)
- Soft shadows, rounded corners (12px-16px border radius)
- Subtle animations (fade-in on scroll, gentle hover states)
- **NO** heavy gradients, **NO** dark mode overload
- White space is sacred — like a well-designed sanctuary

### Navigation Structure (4 items max)
1. **Visit** — Service times, location, what to expect, parking
2. **Groups** — Life groups, men's/women's, youth, young adults
3. **Media** — Sermons, podcasts, livestream, blog
4. **Connect** — Contact, prayer requests, donations, volunteer

### Image Style
- Real warmth: people talking after service, kids in ministry, outdoor shots of Steinbach
- Warm lighting, golden hour tones
- Avoid: staged handshakes, empty pews, corporate headshots
- Unsplash search terms: `church community`, `fellowship`, `steeples`, `worship`, `prayer hands`, `community gathering`

## Copy Direction for Harper
- Tone: Warm, conversational, never corporate
- "Visit us" not "Attend our services"
- "What to Expect" page is critical — first-time visitors care most about:
  - When to show up
  - Where to park
  - What about kids?
  - What's the vibe?
  - What should I wear? (answer: literally anything)
