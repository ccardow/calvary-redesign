## "PLAN YOUR VISIT" PAGE

### What to Expect
We know walking into a new church for the first time takes a little courage. Here's what you'll find at Calvary:

**When to Arrive**
Grab a coffee and a friendly conversation 10–15 minutes before service starts. The doors open early and the coffee's always on.

**Parking**
We have parking right off Main Street. If you're bringing kids, head to our front lot — it's the closest drop-off point to our children's area.

**What About My Kids?**
We've got you covered. During each service, after our opening praise and worship, kids ages 1 through Grade 4 head to their own classrooms with trained volunteers. You can stay and watch the handoff, or drop them and head to the sanctuary — whatever feels right.

**What Should I Wear?**
Whatever you're comfortable in. Jeans and a t-shirt? Great. Sunday best? That works too. You fit right in either way.

**How Long Is Service?**
About 75 minutes — enough time to worship, hear from God's Word, and still make it to lunch with the family.

**Coffee?**
Obviously. We take our coffee very seriously.

## GROUPS PAGE

### Get Connected
Church isn't meant to be a solo journey. At Calvary, we do life together — in small groups, shared meals, honest conversations, and late-night board games.

### Life Groups
Small groups meet throughout the week in homes across Steinbach. It's where deep friendships are built and real life gets lived. Talk to one of our group leaders to find the one that fits your season.

### Harvest (50+)
Golden years, golden community. Our 50+ ministry gathers for fellowship, shared meals, trips, and good conversation. Because wisdom gets better with company.

### Men's Ministry
First Saturday of every month — an evening of fellowship, food, and board games at the church. No sign-up needed. Just show up.

### Women's Ministry
Tuesday mornings, 9–11 AM. We gather for study, prayer, and community. Part of our morning is spent in the prayer room — a quiet space to connect with God and pray for our church and families.

### Young Adults
Finding your way in your 20s? You don't have to do it alone. Our young adults group is a place to ask hard questions, build friendships, and grow in faith with people who get it.

### Youth Group
For teens who are ready to explore faith on their own terms. Fun, honest, and real.
